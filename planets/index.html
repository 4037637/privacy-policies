Every rock you fire becomes a planet. Every planet you ignore becomes a black hole.

Line up bank shots off the walls, watch rocks settle and grow into worlds from tiny Mars to huge Jupiter, and smash them before they collapse into black holes that swallow everything around. Play solo, against a bot, with a friend on the same phone or with players online.
