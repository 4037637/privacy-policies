Privacy Policy — Planets vs Black Holes: Duel

This privacy policy applies to the Planets vs Black Holes: Duel app for Android and iOS, together with its online game server and any related services operated by Hlib Chornenkyi (collectively, the "Application"). Hlib Chornenkyi is the developer of the Application and the controller of your personal data, and is hereby referred to as the "Service Provider". Contact: 4037637@gmail.com.


Playing offline

Single-player games against the computer work without an account and without sending any personal information. No registration is required to play. Your settings (sound, vibration, language and similar) are saved only on your device.


What information does the Application collect and how is it used?

When you open the online part of the Application (online duels, slots, profile), the Application connects to the Service Provider's game server and creates a game account. The following information is processed:

  • Game account identifiers. A random identifier generated on your device and the account identifier on the game server. Used to keep your game profile between sessions.
  • Nickname. A randomly generated nickname, or, if you sign in with Google or Apple, your first name. Shown to other players.
  • Google or Apple sign-in (optional). If you choose to link your Google or Apple account, the server stores the account identifier, your first name and (for Google) your profile photo. Your last name and e-mail address are not stored: the server deletes them immediately after sign-in. Your first name and photo are shown to other players.
  • Profile photo (optional). If you choose your own photo from the camera or gallery, only that photo is uploaded to the game server and shown to other players; the Application does not read your other photos. Before it is accepted, the photo is checked for nudity by an automated model running on the Service Provider's own server; the photo is not sent to any third party for this check. Other players can report a photo; a photo reported several times is hidden until it is reviewed by the Service Provider.
  • Game activity. Online duels you play (moves, scores and results), rating and rank, experience, statistics, friends and blocked players. Your rating, rank and statistics are shown to other players.
  • Fair-play and moderation records. Cancelled matches, wrong room codes and refused or reported photos. Used to apply temporary restrictions against abuse.
  • Diagnostic logs (optional). Only when you press the button to send a log, a technical log of the game session is sent to the server to investigate a problem. It contains the app version, device model and operating system version (as reported by the device's web view), screen size, performance figures and, if you played online recently, that duel's technical data. It does not contain your name or photo.
  • Technical data. Your IP address is used to deliver network traffic and to protect the server. It is not used to identify or track you. It may appear in the server's technical logs, which are kept for a limited time for security and troubleshooting and then deleted.

This information is used only to provide the online game: matchmaking, duels, profiles, rating, friends, moderation of profile photos, protection against abuse and fixing problems. The Application contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking tools, does not use the advertising ID, and does not sell your information or use it for profiling.


Device permissions

The Application uses the Internet connection (online play) and vibration (game effects; can be turned off in the settings). The camera and photo gallery are opened through the system picker only when you choose to set a profile photo. The Application does not access your contacts, location, microphone or files.


Legal bases (EEA, UK and similar laws)

Game account, game activity and diagnostic logs you send are processed to provide the online game you requested (performance of a contract). Google or Apple sign-in and a profile photo are processed on the basis of your consent, which you can withdraw at any time by removing them in the Application. Fair-play records and technical logs are processed on the basis of the Service Provider's legitimate interest in keeping the game fair and the server secure.


Where is the information stored and how is it protected?

The game server is hosted in the European Union (Germany) by the hosting provider Hetzner Online GmbH, which stores the data on behalf of the Service Provider and does not use it for its own purposes. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS). Access to the server is limited to the Service Provider.


How long is the information kept?

Your game account and its data are kept until you delete the account. Records of played duels are kept to ensure fair play; after your account is deleted they no longer contain anything that links them to you. Diagnostic logs are kept only as long as needed to investigate the problem. Technical server logs are kept for a limited time and then deleted.


Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the Application: PROFILE → DELETE ACCOUNT. This deletes your game account, nickname, linked Google or Apple account, profile photo, rating, statistics, friends and all other data tied to the account. You can also request deletion without the Application: see how to delete your account.


Does the Application collect location information?

No. The Application does not collect precise or approximate location of your device.


Do third parties see and/or have access to information obtained by the Application?

No information is shared with or sold to third parties. Other players see your nickname or first name, profile photo, rating, rank and statistics. The hosting provider stores data only on behalf of the Service Provider. If you use Google or Apple sign-in, Google or Apple processes that sign-in under their own privacy policies. Information may be disclosed if required by law.
